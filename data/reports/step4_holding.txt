  WHY THIS HAPPENS -- worth stating, because it is not a bug and it
  materially changes the pool. Two distinct causes:
    (a) LATE ARRIVALS. Most of these members' first purchase falls in
        2019, at the very end of the formation window. They are largely
        the freshman class sworn in January 2019. A high trade count
        does NOT give them a formation track record: their legs only
        start earning at the window's edge, so there is no formation
        performance to rank them on. Ranking them anyway would score
        them on a couple of months of noise.
    (b) SELLERS ONLY. Some members made no PURCHASES in formation at
        all, so they open no long legs. Invested months counts long
        legs, because the long-only construction is what the ranking
        uses.
  Both exclusions are structural, applied before any return was ranked,
  and identical in spirit to the trade-count floor.
